FAQ

What phone do I need?
An iPhone that can use the front and rear cameras at the same time (most models from iPhone XS/XR on), running iOS 17 or later.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. Everything is processed on your phone. There is no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. See the Privacy Policy.

What is the difference between the free and Full versions?
The free version lets you shoot, edit, save and share. The Full Version (a one-time purchase, not a subscription) adds all looks and filters, special stickers, more text styles and Beauty.

I changed phones. How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings in ZiYou Camera and tap Restore Purchases on the first card, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

The cut-out is off, or my phone gets warm.
In Settings, set Person cutout quality to Smooth to save battery and stay cooler. Cut-outs get worse in dim light or when you are very close to the lens.
