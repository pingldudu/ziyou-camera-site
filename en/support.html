Getting started

Requirements, photos and video

What phone do I need?

An iPhone that can use the front and rear cameras at the same time (most models from iPhone XS/XR on), running iOS 17 or later. On other models you will see an “unavailable” message when you open the app.

How do I take my first photo?

On first launch tap Start and allow camera access. The viewfinder shows you and the scenery together: drag or pinch on the person to move and resize yourself, swipe up or down or pinch elsewhere to zoom, tap to focus. Press the shutter to shoot; the thumbnail at the bottom left opens what you just took.

How do I record video?

Switch Photo to Video at the bottom and press the shutter. To record the sound around you, allow the microphone; you can also shoot without it. Video is always full screen.

The viewfinder is black, or it says access is missing.

Open iPhone Settings > ZiYou Camera and make sure Camera access is on. If it says the device is not supported, that iPhone cannot use the front and rear cameras at once.

Shooting and editing

Looks, filters, stickers, text, saving

What is the difference between a Look and a Filter?

A Look sets the whole style of the photo (magazine, cinema, postcard, Polaroid, boarding pass and so on, sometimes with a layout and text); a Filter only adjusts the colour. You can combine them, before or after shooting.

How do I add stickers, text and borders?

Tap the thumbnail at the bottom left of the viewfinder to open the creation. The toolbar below has Look, Filter, Blend, Border, Sticker, Text, Beauty, Adjust and Framing. Stickers and text can be dragged, resized and rotated once placed; there is a limit on how many one creation holds.

How do I save and share?

In the editor tap “Save as new photo” to write to your Photo Library (this needs “Add Photos” access). Sharing uses the system share sheet and the result goes only to the app you choose.

How long are my creations kept?

The in-app album keeps the latest 60; older ones are removed automatically. You can clear the in-app library in Settings with one tap; photos already saved to the system Photo Library are not affected.

Purchases and the Full Version

One-time purchase, restore, offer codes

What is the difference between the free and Full versions?

The free version lets you shoot, edit, save and share in full, and includes basic looks and filters and some stickers and text. The Full Version (a one-time purchase, not a subscription) adds all looks and filters, special stickers, more text styles and Beauty.

How do I buy it?

Tap any feature with a crown, or the first card in Settings, to open the purchase page, tap “One-time purchase” and confirm in the system dialog. You keep it forever and it never renews.

I changed phones or reinstalled. How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in ZiYou Camera and tap Restore Purchases on the first card, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

I have an offer code. How do I redeem it?

Open Settings and tap Redeem Code on the first card. The system redemption sheet opens; enter your code there.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). We cannot refund you directly.

Privacy and data

Photos, face data, location, deleting

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your phone. There is no account, server, ad or analytics. See the Privacy Policy.

Does Beauty collect my face data?

Only when you use Beauty, the app uses Apple's Vision on your phone to find coordinate points for the face contour, eyes, eyebrows and lips. They are not a photo of a face, are not used to identify anyone, are saved with the creation on your phone, and are never uploaded or shared. See the “Face data” section of the Privacy Policy.

How is location used?

It is off by default. It is used only when you turn on “Show location on photos” or add “Altitude” text, to label the place or read the altitude. To look up a place name, the coordinates at that moment are sent to Apple's place-name service; photos are never sent.

How do I delete my data?

We do not have your data. Everything in the app is on your phone: deleting a creation, clearing the library in Settings or deleting the app removes the matching data, face points included.

Troubleshooting

Cut-out, heat, Beauty, saving fails

The cut-out is off, or my phone gets warm.

In Settings set Person cutout quality to Smooth to save battery and run cooler. Cut-outs get worse in dim light or when you are very close to the lens. While recording, the device lowers the resolution automatically to keep things smooth.

Beauty says no face was found.

Beauty needs a clearly visible face in the photo. A very small face, something covering it or dim light can stop it being detected.

Saving fails.

Make sure “Add Photos” access is allowed in iOS Settings, and check the phone's free storage.
