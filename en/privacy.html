Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

In one sentence

ZiYou Camera’s photos, videos and person masks are processed and stored only on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Camera and microphone

The front and rear cameras are used together to place you into the scenery you are shooting. The image is composited only in your phone’s memory. When recording video, if you allow the microphone, the sound around you is saved along with it; you can still record if you don’t allow it.

Photo Library

Photos are written to your Photo Library only when you tap “Save”. The app does not read the photos in your library; “Browse Photo Library” uses the system picker, and your selection is not handed to the app. Creations in the in-app album are kept in the app’s own storage, up to the 60 most recent, and can be cleared in one tap in Settings; they are also deleted when you delete the app.

Location (optional, off by default)

Location is used only when you turn on “Show location on photos” or add an “Altitude” text sticker. To get a place name, the coordinates at that moment are sent to Apple’s place-name service for lookup; the photo is not sent. The place name is stored only in your creation. After you turn the switch off, location is no longer used continuously. When you add “Altitude” text or a sticker, the current altitude is read once: the barometer is used first (this needs the “Motion & Fitness” permission and is supported on iPhone 12 and later), and location is used if it cannot be read. The number is stored only in your creation and is not sent anywhere.

Face data (Beauty)

Only when you use Beauty (skin smoothing, face slimming, bigger eyes and so on), the app uses Apple's built-in Vision framework on your iPhone to find where a face is and its outline: coordinate points for the face contour, eyes, eyebrows and lips. These are used to place the Beauty effect in the right spot. They are just a few sets of coordinate numbers: not a photo of a face, not used to identify anyone, and no face template or faceprint is created. The points are saved together with that creation in the app's own storage (so editing it again does not need to recompute them). They are never uploaded, never sent to us or to any third party, never used for ads, analytics or tracking, and never shared with anyone. When you delete the creation, clear your creations in Settings, when it is removed automatically because it is older than the latest 60, or when you delete the app, its face points are deleted with it. A creation that never used Beauty produces no face data. Like the rest of the app's data, they may be included in your own iPhone backup (for example iCloud Backup); whether to back up is your choice in iOS Settings. The person cut-out used while shooting (separating you from the background) only handles the outline of the person and does not involve face points.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the finished image goes only to the app you choose in the system share sheet (for example WeChat). The app itself does not include any third-party sharing SDK.

Fonts

Some Chinese fonts, such as Clerical Script, Wei Stele and Running Regular Script, are optional fonts in iOS. The first time you choose one, the system downloads the font file from Apple (an internet connection is needed); only the font is downloaded, and no photos or personal information are sent. Fonts that have been downloaded can be used offline afterwards.

Purchases

The Full Version is a one-time purchase (not a subscription), and payment is handled by Apple’s App Store. We cannot see your payment method, Apple ID or orders, and we have no accounts of our own; the app only asks the system “has this Apple ID bought the Full Version?”, to decide whether to unlock it.

What we collect

We collect no personal information. There is no sign-in, no cloud sync, no third-party SDKs, and no data is shared with any third party.

Your choices

At any time you can turn off the camera, microphone, location, Motion & Fitness and Photos permissions in iPhone Settings > ZiYou Camera; you can also turn off the front camera, turn off location display and clear your creations in the app’s own settings.

Children

The app does not collect any data from children of any age group, because it does not collect data at all.
